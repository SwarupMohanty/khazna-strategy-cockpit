  • Contracted MW is ahead of plan (+6%). The gap between contracted and billing MW is the number to watch: revenue follows energisation.
  • FY revenue forecast −7% vs plan, almost entirely timing. Every month of delay on the pipeline costs about the amount shown in the Scenario tab.
  • Decisions needed: re-sequence fit-out at the two delayed sites; confirm pricing floor for the next pre-lease; approve the standard business-case template.

Comparability — three illustrative cases on the same framework

Method: capex 60% in year 0 and 40% in year 1; ready for service after year 1 plus any delay; occupancy ramps in a straight line over the lease-up period; price escalates 2% a year; 20-year horizon, no terminal value; pre-tax, unlevered. Simplified on purpose — a production model adds power pass-through, phasing by hall, financing, tax and terminal value.
